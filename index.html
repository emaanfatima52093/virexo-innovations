About Virexo Innovations

A studio built around one team, start to finish.

Virexo Innovations was founded on a simple idea: digital products fail more often from disconnected handoffs than from bad ideas. We're a technology company that keeps strategy, design and engineering under one roof, working from the same brief, on the same timeline, for the same outcome.

We work with founders, marketing teams and product leads who need a technology partner that can think in business goals, and still ship production-ready code across web, design and digital strategy.

Our mission

Make thoughtful digital products accessible to teams of any size, without the agency overhead.

Who we're for

Founders and product leads who need senior-level execution, not another layer of account management.

How we work

A fixed process, applied to every project.

The same four stages run every engagement, in order — so you always know what's happening next and why.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We map the business goal, the users, and the technical constraints before a single screen gets designed.

  2. 02

    Design

    Wireframes become a working prototype fast, so decisions get made on something you can click through, not a slide deck.

  3. 03

    Build

    The same team that designed it writes the code — clean, tested, and documented for whoever maintains it next.

  4. 04

    Launch & grow

    We ship, then stay close for the first weeks in production, fixing rough edges and tracking what the data says.

Questions

Before you reach out.

The things most founders and product leads ask us in the first call.

How much does a project typically cost?

Most engagements fall between $8,000–$40,000 depending on scope. We scope every project with a fixed quote before work starts, so there are no surprise invoices.

How long does a typical build take?

A marketing site usually ships in 4–6 weeks. Larger product builds run 8–14 weeks. You'll get a week-by-week timeline before we start, and a working prototype in week one.

Do you work with our existing tech stack?

Usually, yes. We build primarily with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React, and integrate with most CMSs and back-ends. If your stack is unusual, we'll flag that during scoping, not after.

What happens after launch?

We stay on for two to four weeks after launch to fix rough edges and watch real usage data. After that, you can hand it to your own team or keep us on a lightweight retainer.

Can you work with a small or early-stage team?

Yes — about half of our clients are early-stage. We scope projects to fit real budgets rather than pushing a one-size agency package.
